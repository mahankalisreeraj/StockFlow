# StockFlow – Inventory Management System

A web-based Inventory Management System designed to help small businesses manage products, purchases, sales, customers, suppliers, and stock efficiently.

## Project Overview

StockFlow aims to simplify inventory management by providing a centralized platform for tracking stock levels and managing daily business operations.

It automatically updates inventory when purchases are received and sales are completed, helping maintain accurate stock records and reduce manual errors.

## Key Features

- **Authentication and Authorization:** Secure login with Admin and Regular User roles.
- **Dashboard:** Overview of sales, purchases, current stock, and low-stock products.
- **Product Management:** Add, view, edit, search, and deactivate products.
- **Category Management:** Organize products into categories.
- **Purchase Management:** Record purchases and update stock when goods are received.
- **Sales Management:** Record sales and prevent sales beyond available stock.
- **Stock Management:** Track stock movements and perform manual adjustments.
- **Customer Management:** Maintain customer details and sales history.
- **Supplier Management:** Maintain supplier details and purchase history.
- **User Management:** Admin-controlled user account management.
